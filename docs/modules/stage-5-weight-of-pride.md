# Stage 5 — The Weight of Pride

Fictional coma nightmare about refusing help. The upper route fails on its own; the lower route only works through cooperation — and the one trapped below is the protagonist himself, so helping him is literally helping yourself. No claim of religious punishment.

**Revision (17 Oct, Umar's feedback):** only the upper route is open at first; its collapse raises the lower path. The trapped figure is a copy of the protagonist and merges into him at the tower gate.

## Spec

- **Layout** (`Assets/HellSlice/Scenes/WeightOfPride.unity`, along +Z, lethal lava below everything): start bank → two routes. **Upper route** (x = −5): stair up to a high bridge leading straight to the lit upper doorway of a ruined tower. **Lower route** (x = 4–11): three platform stones → lower bank with the figure's cell → lava gap → flame causeway → exit landing → tower doorway with the forward gate → completion trigger. The figure's side (x = 11–14: cell, hold switch, gallery, passage door) is behind iron bars the player cannot pass. **The three stones are saved inactive**: before the collapse the lower bank is a 9.6 m unjumpable gap away, so the upper route is the only way.
- **Collapse:** walking onto the bridge stub enters a trigger volume 4 m before the break; every bridge segment ahead loses collision at once and falls into the lava in a cascade, and a solid broken edge (rubble + invisible barrier) appears so no one walks or jumps off it. The stair and stub stay: a safe way back. One memory line shows on the HUD for 8 s, and the lower-path stones rise out of the lava (1.5 s), opening the second route.
- **Cooperation:** E at the cell lever (player side) opens the cell gate. The figure walks fixed points to the hold switch and stays on it; the plate lights, a lit link runs to the gap and the final-route bridge extends across the 6.5 m lava gap. The player cannot reach or operate the switch. Beyond the flames, E at the passage lever (only while the figure holds) opens the figure's passage door; the figure walks its gallery to a fixed spot beside the forward gate. When **both** are inside the exit area, the figure walks into the player, shrinks and vanishes (merge); then the forward gate opens, the next transformation step previews once and the merge line shows; the completion trigger behind the gate commits Stage 5 once. The bridge stays once extended, so retracing never traps the player.
- **Flames:** four contiguous 2 m flame rows (`SliceHazard`, 7 s cycle, offsets 0 / 0.8 / 1.6 / 2.4 s) burn as a wave rolling toward the player; touching a burning row is lethal (Damage 5). When the nearest row goes out, all four are cool and a walking crossing has ≈0.7 s of slack — possible without Q; with Q (slow time) the slack is several seconds. Safe ground before and after.
- **Figure = the protagonist:** its visuals are copies of the player's 8 body parts with the same materials, so it shares the player's runtime material and changes appearance with him. Simple scripted mover between fixed points (no colliders, no AI, never blocks or gets stuck), 4 m/s on world time (Q slows it like other world objects); the final merge homes on the player at 7 m/s (faster than walking or dodging) so it always arrives.
- **Rules:** StageNumber 5, SlowTimeUnlockStage 3 (Q available). Space jump, Shift dodge unchanged. No projection, new ability or combat. Death (lava, fall, flames) reloads the scene: figure, collapse, levers, bridge, doors, gate and timers reset; earlier completed stages survive. NextScene empty (no Stage 6 yet).
- **No bypass:** collapse trigger cannot be jumped past (5 m tall, polled every frame); the gap is wider than run + jump + dodge reach (≈5.3 m); bars are continuous 3 m colliders; the tower facade has no way around the gate; completion code also requires the gate to have opened, which requires the figure to have arrived and the player in the exit area.
- **Lines (story proposal, pending approval):** collapse "I thought asking for help made me small."; merge "The one I helped was me." — Inspector fields `Memory` and `MergeLine`; clear either to show nothing.
